💧 Irrigation Techniques

Drip Irrigation

Delivers water directly to plant roots and reduces water waste.

Sprinkler Irrigation

Sprays water over crops like natural rainfall.

Surface Irrigation

Water flows across agricultural land using gravity.

🌍 Soil Information

Clay Soil

Holds water and nutrients. Suitable for crops such as rice.

Sandy Soil

Drains water quickly. Suitable for groundnut and some root crops.

Loamy Soil

A balanced soil type suitable for many agricultural crops.

  • Use quality seeds for better production.

  • Use fertilizers according to soil test recommendations.
